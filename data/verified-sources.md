# FISAT fact and asset register

Verified against the official FISAT site on 7 October 2026. This file is an editorial/source guide, not a claim that any current notice remains valid after that date. Before changing time-sensitive numbers, routes or dates, check the linked live source again. Public programme eligibility, schedules and access rules are always handed off to FISAT rather than inferred here.

## Institution, values and credentials

- [The Institution](https://fisat.ac.in/institution/) states FISAT was established in 2002 by the Federal Bank Officers’ Association Educational Society; UGC granted ten-year autonomous status in 2025; NAAC A+ in the second cycle with a score of 3.45 CGPA; six B.Tech programmes accredited by NBA; ISO 21001:2018 certification; KTU affiliation; and AICTE approval. It lists seven B.Tech programmes, MBA, MCA, Integrated MCA, five M.Tech programmes and doctoral programmes in various disciplines.
- [Vision and Mission](https://fisat.ac.in/vision/) is the source for the two vision/mission statements reproduced on About.
- The [official home page](https://fisat.ac.in/) displays 3,200+ students and ₹12 Cr+ in scholarships; these are presented with source attribution.
- The [Campus Life page](https://fisat.ac.in/campus-life/) identifies a 40-acre campus.
- [Governing Body](https://fisat.ac.in/governing-body/) and [The Principal](https://fisat.ac.in/principal/) are the sources for the names and titles shown in the About leadership section.
- The [student portal page](https://fisat.ac.in/stud-portal/) describes student profile, semester attendance, sessional marks and examination results and its login credentials; the redesign links to that page and does not collect those credentials.

## Campus and facilities

It describes an auditorium with seating for 800 and audio/stage facilities, as well as a 30-bus conveyance fleet. Only display those figures with the source and remind users to verify current service and access details.

## Research and MoUs

- The live [Research and Development Cell page](https://fisat.ac.in/college-research-cell/) records that an institution-level Interdisciplinary Research Cell began in 2018, later functioned as the College Research Cell and was renamed the R&D Cell in 2023. It describes research/innovation culture, coordination of faculty/research-scholar work, interdisciplinary research/consultancy, grant-proposal help, a Project Bank, Ph.D. and funded-project reviews, patent-filing guidance, research seminars/workshops, proposals for a new research centre, reporting and ethical oversight.
- The R&D page says the Cell “explores the proposals for establishing a new research centre”; it does not supply an authoritative list of operational research centres or current project, patent or publication totals. The redesign makes this limitation explicit instead of claiming outputs.
- [MoUs and Collaborations](https://fisat.ac.in/mous-and-collaborations/) is organized by Civil, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Instrumentation, Mechanical, Business Administration, Computer Applications, and Science & Humanities.
