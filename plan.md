# FISAT Website Redesign — Plan

## Product and implementation

Build a genuinely multi-page, static-content institutional website for Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), Angamaly, Kerala, not a single long landing page. Use Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4 via PostCSS, Motion (`motion`), and Lucide React. Use typed, structured content modules independent of UI. Keep the application frontend-only: no user accounts, private records, or backend are required. Department and secondary-facility pages are statically generated from the verified records with `generateStaticParams` and `dynamicParams = false`, so unknown slugs reach the shared 404 rather than a server fallback. Client components are limited to navigation, search/filtering, tabs/accordions, campus explorer, carousels and restrained motion. SEO metadata is generated per route in static HTML. A real public origin is not yet configured, so do not guess canonical/`og:url` addresses from Preview or claim the official domain for the redesign. The Manus Preview serves the current `out/` static export on the configured port (3000), so review uses the same HTML and branded 404 behavior as the declared static build. The website’s factual source of truth is the official FISAT site. Where published official statistics disagree, omit the ambiguous metric instead of making an unsupported choice.

## Project structure

- `app/`: App Router shared root layout, route pages, global styles and route-specific metadata for home, about, academics, departments, faculty, admissions, campus life, facility hub and dedicated facility/detail routes, research, placements, student life, news, events and contact.
- `components/`: reusable navigation, heroes, breadcrumbs, cards, galleries, statistics, timelines, filters, tabs, accordions, campus explorer and footer.
- `data/`: typed programmes, departments, facilities, the full public faculty roster, news/events, placements, research and contact content, with source notes for facts.
- `data/assets.ts` and Manus project storage (`/manus-storage/...`): authenticated, optimized FISAT photography used by the site; retain official image provenance and do not portray generated images as the real campus.
- `public/manus-routes.json`: synchronized page-route manifest.
- `plan.md`, `TODO.md`: approved decisions and outcome-based work items.

## Design system

- **Design Movement:** premium academic editorial / contemporary Kerala institutional design, with cinematic photography and restrained digital craft.
- **Core Principles:** institutional confidence; human-scale storytelling; clear, useful wayfinding; credible detail over decoration.
- **Color Philosophy:** follow the user's updated palette: black `#000000`, white `#FFFFFF` and signature blue `#2C3480`. Use black for confident editorial grounding, white for clarity and breathing room, and the single indigo-blue accent for calls to action, active states and wayfinding; supporting grays and pale blue tints are limited to readable surfaces, dividers and text. Remove the earlier gold accent and unrelated chromatic colors.
- **Layout Paradigm:** editorial, image-led, asymmetric sections and bento arrangements; use wide margins and variable composition rather than a uniform centered template.
- **Signature Elements:** FISAT’s authentic official wordmark in the navbar (never redraw or misrepresent it); fine blue editorial rules with numbered chapter markers; a photo-led campus-explorer interaction with blue location pins on dark imagery.
- **Interaction Philosophy:** the interaction should aid discovery and navigation, not compete with academic content; preserve keyboard access and touch-sized targets.
- **Animation:** Motion-based hero image expansion and layered copy entrance, animated count-up, a quiet blue reading-progress line, staggered card/timeline arrivals, hover/focus feedback, active campus-marker pulse, menu, tab and page transitions. Keep interface transitions within 300–500 ms; the route shell, selected facility panel and campus-location details must be visible in initial static HTML before hydration and remain readable if JavaScript is unavailable. Play route-entry and selection transitions only after hydration or user input, preserve keyboard/touch access, honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, and use no WebGL, autoplay video or perpetual ambient movement.
- **21st.dev Pattern References:** adapt, rather than copy, the [Scroll Reveal Image](https://21st.dev/@unlumen/components/scroll-reveal-image) image-width/scale treatment for the FISAT hero, the [Bento Grid](https://21st.dev/@designali-in/components/bento-grid) asymmetric visual hierarchy for campus content, and [Transition Panel](https://21st.dev/@ibelick/components/transition-panel/with-tabs) for short, reduced-motion-aware section changes. Build the experiences as semantic project components using authentic FISAT images; do not reuse the references’ stock visuals or default styling.
- **Typography System:** Instrument Serif for editorial display headings, paired with a neutral Inter sans-serif body; large high-contrast headlines, readable line lengths and a clear responsive hierarchy.
- **Brand Essence:** engineering education at the intersection of academic craft and a connected campus community; personality: **ambitious, grounded, human**.
- **Brand Voice:** concise, confident, invitational; example headlines: “Build what comes next.” and “More than a campus. A place to become.”
- **Wordmark & Logo:** use the official FISAT logo/wordmark from the institution for recognizable and accurate identity; supporting geometric forms may echo engineering and campus architecture without impersonating an official seal.
- **Signature Brand Color:** FISAT Blue `#2C3480`.

## Page and content system

All pages use the shared responsive navigation, distinct page hero, breadcrumb, contextual navigation as useful, and shared footer. The mega menu includes all requested academics, campus-life and facilities destinations; the mobile version is a full-screen drawer. Implement distinct page compositions: cinematic home, editorial institution story, filterable academic explorer and directory, usable admissions steps, immersive campus-life narrative, asymmetric facility bento, utility-forward transport route explorer, editorial research/placement pages, publication-style news, event discovery and split contact/map. Build the specified dedicated content pages and individual pages for major facilities; department profiles share a data-driven template with sticky section navigation and tabs. Maintain accessible search/filter, active states, accordions, carousels and mobile swipe interactions.

The home page uses the approved “BUILD WHAT COMES NEXT.” hero, official campus imagery, the brief’s copy and CTAs; verified legacy/student/scholarship information; an editorial campus introduction; programmes; asymmetric campus bento; research spotlight; placement callout; editorial news; and a final application CTA. The signature campus explorer identifies Main Block, Library, Hostels, Cafeteria, Gym, Sports, Labs and Auditorium with keyboard- and pointer-selectable markers and contextual location cards over real FISAT campus imagery. This photo-led visual explorer is descriptive rather than a measured or navigable site plan.

Data must be taken from official FISAT pages. Verified examples include 23 years, ₹12 Cr+ scholarships and 3,200+ students on the official home page; 602 placement offers dated May 1, 2026 is displayed on official pages alongside inconsistent 2026 numbers. Since the approved plan is to omit ambiguous statistics, do not publish the conflicting 2026 offers/highest-package figures until reconciled. Official transport material currently states a fleet of 30 buses, but do not invent routes or timings. Official hostel material states four blocks and accommodation for 1300+ students; include these only with attribution/current source link. Official PG courses and contact/admission information are taken from the live official pages. Never invent people, faculty details, routes, event dates, accreditation credentials or research claims; use a transparent “See FISAT’s official page” handoff where a live/current answer is not verified.

## Project quality attributes

Responsive at 360, 390, 430, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 px; accessible semantic structure, ARIA where needed, keyboard navigation, visible focus, contrast, alt text and reduced motion. Optimize authentic campus images as WebP/AVIF when supported, use lazy loading/responsive sizing and avoid large dependencies or third-party blocking services. Preserve short pathfinding: admissions, programmes, departments, faculty, hostel, cafeteria, transport, placements, facilities and contact within one or two navigation actions. Keep the route manifest synchronized with App Router routes. Each indexable page has route-specific initial-HTML title/description/Open Graph/Twitter data and real page content. Omit canonical and `og:url` until a true public URL is explicitly configured; do not emit an internal Preview-derived origin.
